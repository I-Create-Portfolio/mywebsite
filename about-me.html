GORDWIN ODHIAMBO (b.1993) is a Kenyan contemporary photographer and artist born and raised in Nairobi, Kenya. His photography and life is shaped mostly by the work he has done in Nairobi, especially in Kibera slums.

He explores people's determination, and their inextricable connection to where they live - with these, he delves into the endearing intricasies of the everyday, and the plights faced by those living on the margins while navigating the confines of urban life.

His practice involves visiting a place many times until he sees the changes in real time.

Gordwin is a Fellow with DW Akademie. He has attended several advanced photojournalism masterclasses with VII Photo Academy, NOOR Images, World Press Photo Foundation among others. In 2024, he was part of the New York Times portfolio (Photography) review.

He strings for AFP, and contributes to Everyday Africa, Natgeoyourshort, and he's also a member of the African Photojournalism Database.

Assignments include with Bloomberg, National Geographic, NYTimes, Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation, Acumen, Open Society Foundation, and many others.

Gordwin is available for assignments anywhere on earth
